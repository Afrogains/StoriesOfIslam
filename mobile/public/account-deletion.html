Delete your Stories of Islam account

  1. Open Stories of Islam and select Library.
  2. In the account panel, choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm the permanent deletion.

This removes the Keycloak identity, synchronized bookmarks, and listening progress. Operational backup copies expire through the normal encrypted 35-day rotation. Editorial records not linked to a listener account are retained.

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@storiesofislam.example from the account email. Replace this example address with a monitored production mailbox before launch.
