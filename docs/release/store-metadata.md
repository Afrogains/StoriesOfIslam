# Store metadata draft

## Name and subtitle

**Stories of Islam**  
Authentic stories, thoughtful listening

## Short description

Explore reviewed stories of the Prophets, Seerah, Companions, and early Muslim
history in English and Arabic, with synchronized listening and a
family-friendly Kids Mode.

## Full description

Stories of Islam brings a carefully organized library of Islamic narratives to
web, iPhone, iPad, and Android. Explore bilingual text, documented sources,
authenticity labels, and reviewed audio. Save stories, continue listening across
devices, and use offline audio on mobile.

Sections include Qisas al-Anbiya, Seerah and Shama’il, the Sahabah, and
historical gleanings. Kids Mode offers a warmer, family-friendly presentation
inside the same general-audience learning app.

Accounts are optional for public reading and listening. Sign in only when you
want synchronized bookmarks and playback progress. Account deletion is
available in the app.

AI may assist the private editorial drafting process. No AI script or
synthesized audio is public until it receives qualified human review.

## Keywords

Islamic stories, Seerah, Prophets, Sahabah, hadith, Arabic, Muslim learning,
audio stories

## Review notes

- General-audience educational app with a family-friendly mode; not submitted
  to the child-directed/Kids category.
- Demo reviewer credentials must be supplied through the private store portal,
  never committed.
- Account deletion: Library → account panel → Delete account.
- Microphone, contacts, location, camera, and photo permissions are not used.
- Background audio is used for user-initiated story playback.

## Required localized assets

- 6.7-inch iPhone, 13-inch iPad, Android phone, and Android tablet screenshots.
- Home, Explore, synchronized reader, The Names, Library sync, and Kids Mode.
- English and Arabic screenshots/descriptions after Arabic copy review.
- Privacy, terms, support, correction, and account-deletion HTTPS URLs.
