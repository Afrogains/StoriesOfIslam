Stories of Islam privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Accounts are optional. When you sign in, we process your Keycloak account identifier, email, bookmarks, and listening progress so they can sync across devices.

Providers and use

MySQL application data, Keycloak authentication, and MinIO media storage are hosted on HostAfrica infrastructure. The separately hosted API processes requests and security logs. Authorized editorial workflows may send reviewed source text to AI and text-to-speech providers; public listening activity is not sent to those providers.

Retention and deletion

Bookmarks and progress remain until removed. Operational logs are normally retained for 30 days. Delete your account and synced data in Library → Delete account; encrypted backups expire through the normal 35-day rotation.

Children

This is a general-audience learning app with a family-friendly Kids Mode, not a child-directed store category. Accounts are optional and parental supervision is encouraged.

Contact

Email privacy@storiesofislam.example. This example address must be replaced with the monitored production contact before launch.
